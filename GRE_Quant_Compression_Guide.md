## What This Is (and What It Isn’t)

This is not a study guide.
It’s a compression artifact.

The GRE Quantitative section does not test mathematical depth. It tests whether you can recognize common problem structures quickly and apply the correct procedure under time pressure. Most test-takers already know the underlying math. What they lack is fast recall, procedural confidence, and resistance to traps.

This document exists to compress the GRE Quant syllabus into a set of recognizable patterns, decision rules, and minimal procedures that can be executed quickly and reliably. If you are looking for theory, proofs, or comprehensive coverage, this is the wrong resource. If you want to stop burning time remembering how to solve things you already learned years ago, this is exactly the point.

How to Use This Document

Each section focuses on a specific class of GRE Quant problems and is structured around three questions:

1. What kind of question is this, really?

2. What is the fastest correct approach?

3. What mistake is the test trying to bait you into making?

You should read each section actively, then immediately drill that exact problem type until recognition becomes automatic. The act of constructing and refining these explanations is intentional: writing forces compression, and compression is what turns dormant knowledge into usable skill under time constraints.

This document is intentionally basic, incomplete and opinionated. It prioritizes frequency, speed, and return on effort over elegance or exhaustiveness. That is not a flaw. That is the point.
